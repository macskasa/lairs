# Lairs Tracker v0.20

Released 21 November 2026

## New

- **Choose the cubes that pay for dice.** When you face a monster or trap in Solo, the cubes that will pay for your dice are outlined on the mat. At first that's the app's usual choice: Conserved first, Quickness last.
  - **Changing it:** tap a cube to add it or drop it.
  - **Conserved cubes:** one can pay for two dice. Tap it twice and it shows ×2; a third tap drops it.
  - **−** and **+** still change the number of dice in the usual order. **Fight** spends exactly the outlined cubes.

## Changed

- **No more keyboard for numbers.** These three are no longer text boxes, so the on-screen keyboard never opens:
  - A monster's new target in Solo.
  - A monster's power in the tracker.
  - The treasure score at the end of a Solo game.
- **How they work instead:** **−** lowers the number by 1 and **+3** raises it by 3. An empty value starts at 3.
  - **A monster's new target** starts at the last target the app knows, so after a fight you usually just tap **−** a few times.
- **Shorter fight text in Solo**, so it fits small phones: "Each die costs a cube. Tap cubes to choose which." The **Fight** button still says "Fight: fail" when you buy no dice.

## Fixed

- **Tracker's power buttons:** they no longer listen to Solo's buttons as well. This had no visible effect yet.

## Tested on

- **Galaxy S24 Ultra and Galaxy S22 Ultra:**
  - As an installed app, with gesture and button navigation.
  - In a Chrome tab.
  - In landscape.
- **iPhone 15 and iPhone SE.**
- **3:4 tablet** (768×1024), both ways.
- **16:9 screen** (1280×720), both ways.

**What was checked:**
- **Layout:** every screen above, with Big cubes on and off. Every encounter step fits without scrolling, including on iPhone SE.
- **Light and dark mode:** paying for dice (including ×2), the −/+3 boxes in Solo and the tracker.
- **Scripted runs:**
  - Choosing cubes for dice, and what Fight then spends.
  - The −/+3 boxes for a monster's target, a monster's power and the treasure score.
  - The full Solo game, the Solo rules, saving and loading.
  - The tracker's rules, and the score.
