# Lairs Tracker v0.18

Released 21 November 2026

## New

- **See what's under your tracker.** When the tracker stands on a square with something on it (a trap, monster, chest, the Start or the Exit), it fades to a soft halo and the square's icon is drawn on top. The last move's line stops at the halo's edge. The line next to your position also says what's there, for example "Trap (eliminated)" or "Monster, target 3". This works in the tracker and in Solo.
- **Card actions in Solo.** A **Cards** button offers abilities from cards you hold. The app can't see your cards, so they're always available:
  - **Look into a space:** spend your Perception cube to learn about any unknown space. You don't move.
  - **Builder reveals one:** the app reveals an unknown space that has something on it, chosen at random.
  - **On the score:** the number of card actions you used is shown with your score and your best score, as par doesn't include them.
- **Choose the cube for a strike.** After **Fatigue a cube** or **Spend a cube**, tap the cube on the mat (the ones that qualify are outlined), or **Any** to let the app pick.

## Changed

- **Solo's Game tab** now looks like the tracker's: Save game, Load game and Give up, then New solo lair and Copy code, with the lair's code, difficulty and par underneath.
- **Installed app settings:** the app's details (full screen, rotation) are always fetched fresh, so Chrome sees changes as soon as it checks the installed app.
  - **When it applies:** Chrome checks at most once a day, while the app is closed and the phone is charging and on Wi-Fi. Reinstalling applies it at once.

## Tested on

- **Galaxy S24 Ultra and Galaxy S22 Ultra:**
  - As an installed app, with gesture and button navigation.
  - In a Chrome tab.
  - In landscape.
- **iPhone 15 and iPhone SE.**
- **3:4 tablet** (768×1024), both ways.
- **16:9 screen** (1280×720), both ways.

**What was checked:**
- **Layout:** the solo screen fits without scrolling on every screen above, including the card row and choosing a cube. The halo was checked in light and dark mode.
- **Scripted runs:**
  - Card actions, choosing the cube, the full solo game, saving and loading.
  - The outlines after every move.
  - The tracker's movement, backtrack and progress rules.
  - Make a lair.
