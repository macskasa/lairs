# Lairs Tracker v0.19

Released 21 November 2026

## New

- **Fat fingers settings.** Settings has a new **Fat fingers** card for easier tapping. It works in Solo for now.
  - **Confirm moves on the map** moved here from the old Solo card.
  - **Big cubes:** the cube mat shows two rows of two baskets, with cubes 22 pixels across instead of 16. A tap in the gap between two cubes still picks one.
  - **Fitting the screen:** in the installed app on the Galaxy S24 Ultra and S22 Ultra, the map keeps its size. In a Chrome tab and on small phones, the map gives up a little height so nothing scrolls. Turned sideways, the baskets stay in one row and the cubes wrap.

## Changed

- **Every New game button confirms the same way.** The first tap turns it into "Tap again to clear" for 3 seconds, and the second tap goes ahead.
  - **Solo:** this replaces the pop-up, on both the Game tab and the start screen. Solo's button is now called **New game** too.
  - **Backing out:** your solo game is only replaced once you start the new lair, so leaving the difficulty page keeps it.
- **Tracker's Game tab:** "Your game saves itself as you play" now sits on its own line under the buttons, so it no longer gets squeezed when New game is waiting for its second tap.

## Fixed

- **Solo score:** 3 monsters are worth 4 points. They were wrongly counted as 6. 2 monsters are still worth 2.
- **Tracker opens on Move:** opening the tracker from the start screen always shows Move. Before, tapping a difficulty or a solo tab could leave the tracker with no tab open. Coming back from Settings keeps the tab you were on.
- **Dark mode difficulty:** on the New solo lair and Make a lair pages, the chosen difficulty was hard to see in dark mode. It now has an accent outline and accent text in both themes.
- **New solo lair page order:** the difficulty is above the Start exploring button again.

## Tested on

- **Galaxy S24 Ultra and Galaxy S22 Ultra:**
  - As an installed app, with gesture and button navigation.
  - In a Chrome tab.
  - In landscape.
- **iPhone 15 and iPhone SE.**
- **3:4 tablet** (768×1024), both ways.
- **16:9 screen** (1280×720), both ways.

**What was checked:**
- **Layout:** every screen above, with Big cubes on and off. The solo screen and every encounter step fit without scrolling. The armed New game buttons fit in one line.
- **Dark mode:** the difficulty pickers, the Fat fingers card and the armed New game buttons, in light and dark.
- **Scripted runs:**
  - Every way into the tracker opens on Move.
  - The New game buttons in both modes.
  - The score with 1, 2 and 3 monsters.
  - The full solo game, choosing a cube for a strike (with Big cubes too), saving and loading.
