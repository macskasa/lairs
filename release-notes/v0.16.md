# Lairs Tracker v0.16

Released 21 November 2026

## New

- **Solo.** Explore a hidden lair on your own. The app builds the lair and acts as the Builder; you play with your own cards, dice and treasures.
  - **Starting:** on the start screen, **Solo → New solo lair**. Pick Apprentice, Journeyman or Guild Master, or play a lair code. Codes are shared with Make a lair, so you can explore a lair a friend made. The lair uses your Lair pieces from Settings.
  - **A dark map:** unknown squares stay dark. When you learn about a space, the app reveals its walls and what's in it, the way the Builder would describe it. The unknown squares you can reach next get a dashed outline.
  - **Actions:**
    - **Creep:** tap a neighbouring square or use the compass.
    - **Peer:** the button in the middle of the compass. You learn about a neighbouring unknown space, then choose whether to creep in for free.
    - **Backtrack:** tap a known square further away. The rulebook rule applies.
  - **Encounters with your own cards:** the app never asks for your cards' numbers, only how things went.
    - **Unknown monster or trap:** it strikes. Apply the strike from your card, then fight or disarm it, or move away.
    - **Known monster or trap:** you must fight or disarm it at once.
    - **A monster that survives:** you enter its new target, which is shown on the square.
  - **Achievements and exiting:** chests opened, monsters defeated and traps disarmed are shown next to the tabs. The Exit lets you out once you have 3 chests, 3 monsters, or 2 of each.
  - **The end:** when you escape or give up, the whole lair is shown, and you can play it again or start a new one.
  - **Kept apart:** your solo game saves itself, separately from your game with a rival. There's no Undo in solo.
- **The start screen's Solo card** shows your solo game in progress, or how your last one ended.

## Tested on

- **Galaxy S24 Ultra and Galaxy S22 Ultra:**
  - As an installed app, with gesture and button navigation.
  - In a Chrome tab.
  - In landscape.
- **iPhone 15 and iPhone SE.**
- **3:4 tablet** (768×1024), both ways.
- **16:9 screen** (1280×720), both ways.

**What was checked:**
- **Layout:** the start screen, New solo lair, the solo screen (exploring, an encounter, the Game tab, the end of the game), Make a lair and the tracker fit without scrolling on every screen above.
- **A full solo game:** a scripted run played through walls, backtracks, peering, a monster that survives and must be fought again, a failed trap and its teleport to the Start, chests, an exit attempt that was too early, escaping, giving up, playing again and reloading the app.
- **Earlier rules:** the tracker's movement, backtrack and progress rules and Make a lair were re-checked.
