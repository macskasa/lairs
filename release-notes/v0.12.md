# Lairs Tracker v0.12

Released 20 November 2026

## New

- **Settings page.** The gear button at the top right opens Settings. The Map button, or the phone's Back button, returns to the map.
- **Game progress.** Settings shows the 12 packages in the order they're opened: Box 0, Box 1, Envelopes A and B, Box 2, Envelopes C and D, Box 3, Envelopes E and F, Box 4 and the Vault. Tap the last one you've opened.
  - Progress belongs to the device, so it stays across new games.
  - It's stored in save files and printed in the PDF header.
  - Loading a save made at another step doesn't change your progress.
  - Rules tied to progress will come in later versions.
- **Tablets and landscape.**
  - Portrait tablets get a wider layout sized to the screen height, with a bigger map and bigger controls.
  - In landscape, on tablets and on phones turned sideways, the map fills the height on the left and the title and controls sit on the right.
  - Phones in portrait look the same as before.
- **README.md** in the repository, covering installation, how to use the app, the rules, and notes for development.
- **Release notes** for every version from now on, in this folder.

## Changed

- **Lair pieces** moved from the Game tab to Settings, and its rows are easier to tap there. The Game tab now holds Save game, Load game, Export PDF and New game.
- **Route button:** on tablets it gets its own full-width row and shows more of the route.

## Good to know

- New installs start at **Box 0**. If you've already opened more, set your progress once in Settings.

## Tested on

- **Galaxy S24 Ultra and Galaxy S22 Ultra:**
  - As an installed app, with gesture and button navigation.
  - In a Chrome tab.
  - In landscape.
- **iPhone 15 and iPhone SE.**
- **3:4 tablet** (768×1024), portrait and landscape.
- **16:9 screen** (1280×720), landscape and portrait.

On every screen the map and controls fit without scrolling. The movement and map rules from v0.10 and v0.11 were re-checked with a scripted run.
