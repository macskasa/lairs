# Lairs Tracker v0.17

Released 21 November 2026

## New in Solo

- **Cube mat.** The Explore tab now has your cubes: Conserved (3 slots), Fresh, Spent and Fatigued. There are 4 basic cubes, a Quickness cube and a Perception cube, and they all start Fresh.
  - **Paying is automatic.** Conserved cubes go first, then basic cubes; the Quickness cube is kept for last, as only it can pay for a backtrack.
  - **Moving a cube by hand:** tap a cube, then a basket. Use this for treasure abilities or corrections.
- **Costs from the rulebook:**
  - **Creep:** any 1 cube.
  - **Peer:** your Perception cube, or any 2.
  - **Backtrack:** your Quickness cube.
  - **Conserve:** up to 3 Fresh cubes, offered when you end your turn. Each can be spent twice next turn.
- **Hustle is a commitment.**
  - **Paying:** tap Hustle and pay first. If you have both, you choose between 2 cubes and your Quickness cube.
  - **Moving:** you then get up to 3 moves. Entering any space that isn't empty ends it, and the rest are lost.
- **Turns and par.**
  - **Turns:** a turn ends when your Fresh cubes run out, or when you tap End turn. Each new turn refreshes your cubes as the rulebook says.
  - **Par:** every lair has one, worked out from 48 simulated explorers who don't know the lair, playing by the cube rules with the real dice. The same lair always has the same par.
- **Strikes and checks.**
  - **Strikes:** apply each one with Fatigue a cube or Spend a cube, or tap a specific cube on the mat.
  - **Checks:** fights and disarms buy dice at a cube each, then you tap the outcome. Buying no dice is a fail.
- **Score.**
  - **Treasures:** at the end you enter your treasure total.
  - **The total:** treasures, plus monsters (the 2nd is worth 2, the 3rd 4 more), plus exit points.
  - **Exit points:** 4 within par, 2 on the turn after, none later. The app tells you once when you're past par, and you can keep exploring or give up.
  - **Best score:** your best on each lair code is kept.
- **Save game and Load game** on the solo Game tab, with the same file-name box as the tracker. A rival save loaded in Solo, or a solo save loaded in the tracker, tells you where it belongs.
- **Confirm moves on the map** (Settings → Solo). When it's on, a tap selects a square and shows what it would do and cost; tap it again or tap the button to go. It's handy on small screens.

## Changed

- **No compass in solo.** You move by tapping the map, and Peer, Hustle and End turn have their own buttons. The tracker keeps its compass.
- **Outlines in solo:** only the unknown squares you could peer or creep into right now are outlined.
- **The installed app runs full screen.** The phone's status and navigation bars are hidden; swipe in from an edge to see them. It also turns with your phone when auto-rotate is on.
  - **Getting the change:** your installed app picks this up after Chrome's next check of the app's details, at most once a day. Reinstalling applies it at once.

- **A scripted solo game:**
  - Every cost, Hustle with its payment choice, Conserve and conserved cubes being spent first.
  - Strikes, buying dice, a surviving monster's new target, a fight you can't skip, a fail with no dice, and a trap's free move to the Start.
  - Confirm moves, escaping and scoring, best scores, playing again and the past-par note.
- **Saved games:** saving and loading, and a solo game saved by v0.16.
- **Make a lair:** every lair code still builds exactly the same lair as before.
- **Earlier tests:** the tracker and Make a lair tests passed again.
