# Lairs Tracker v0.15

Released 20 November 2026

## New

- **Start screen.** The app now opens by asking how you're playing:
  - **With a rival:**
    - **Track my map** opens the tracker with your current game, and shows how far you've got.
    - **Make a lair** builds a random lair for your rival.
  - **Solo:** coming soon.
  - **Getting back:** the house button at the top left of the tracker returns to the start screen. The phone's Back button goes back one screen.
- **Make a lair.** For quick games, the Builder can skip designing and copy a random lair onto their Lair board.
  - **The rules:** every lair follows the rulebook's three lair-building rules, using your Lair pieces from Settings.
  - **Three difficulties:**
    - **Apprentice:** open, every goal reachable without passing a hazard.
    - **Journeyman:** at least one goal behind a hazard.
    - **Guild Master:** every wall, long corridors, at least two goals behind a hazard.
  - **Alike within a difficulty:** each lair is scored by a simulated explorer and only kept if its score falls in that difficulty's range. Layouts are random and turned or mirrored, so there's nothing to memorise.
  - **Lair codes** such as `J1-7KQ3M`: the same code always builds the same lair on any phone. Share it, or use **Enter code** to build it again.
  - **Hidden by default:** the lair opens covered until you tap it, and **Hide** covers it again.
  - **Where to find it:** from the start screen, or **Game → Make a lair**.

## Fixed

- **Example map:** with the note above the map, the tracker no longer scrolls slightly on shorter phones, portrait tablets and phones in landscape.

## Tested on

- **Galaxy S24 Ultra and Galaxy S22 Ultra:**
  - As an installed app, with gesture and button navigation.
  - In a Chrome tab.
  - In landscape.
- **iPhone 15 and iPhone SE.**
- **3:4 tablet** (768×1024), both ways.
- **16:9 screen** (1280×720), both ways.

**What was checked:**
- **Layout:** the start screen, Make a lair and the tracker fit without scrolling on every screen above, in light and dark mode.
- **Lairs:** a scripted run checked several hundred generated lairs against the three lair-building rules, and confirmed that the same code builds the same lair in the browser and in Node.
- **Earlier rules:** the movement, backtrack and progress rules were re-checked.
