# Lairs Tracker v0.22

Released 21 November 2026

## New

- **Hungarian (Magyar).** The app can now be used in Hungarian.
  - **Switching:** tap **EN | HU** at the top of the start screen, or use **Settings → Language**. Until you pick one, the app follows your phone's language.
  - **What's translated:**
    - Tracker: the Move, Map and Game tabs, the map tools, messages, dialogs and the route.
    - Make a lair and Settings.
    - The start screen.
    - Saving, loading and the PDF.
  - **Solo:** still mostly in English for now.
  - **Start and Exit letters:** on the map they become B (Bejárat) and K (Kijárat). The compass reads É, Ny, K, D.
  - **Terms:** the Hungarian names of the game's terms were agreed with the players. Journeyman is Kalandozó; cubes are akciókockák and dice are kockák.
  - **The PDF:** it uses ö and ü in place of ő and ű, because its built-in fonts don't have them.

## Tested on

- **Galaxy S24 Ultra and Galaxy S22 Ultra:**
  - As an installed app, with gesture and button navigation.
  - In a Chrome tab.
  - In landscape.
- **iPhone 15 and iPhone SE.**
- **3:4 tablet** (768×1024), both ways.
- **16:9 screen** (1280×720), both ways.

**What was checked:**
- **Layout in Hungarian and English:** every screen above. Some Hungarian texts were shortened so the start screen, the tool labels, the Game tab buttons (including New game waiting for its second tap) and Make a lair's buttons fit without scrolling or cutting off.
- **No English left:** the tracker's screens, Make a lair and Settings were checked for leftover English.
- **Scripted runs in both languages:** the tracker's movement rules and saving and loading. Switching back to English restores every text.
- **English only:** the full Solo game, its rules, the New game buttons, the power box and the check that the same code gives the same lair.
