# Lairs Tracker v0.21

Released 21 November 2026

## Changed

- **Par is tougher.** Par used to be the middle result of 48 simulated explorers who don't know the lair, and a thinking player usually beat it by about 2 turns. It's now what the **fastest quarter** of them needed.
  - **How much lower:** about 2 turns on average.
  - **Still fixed:** the same lair always gets the same par.
  - **A solo game you already started** keeps the par it began with.
  - **Best scores start a new list,** because older ones were scored against the higher par.
- **Strikes: several cubes in one go.** After **Fatigue a cube** or **Spend a cube**, every cube you tap is applied and the choice stays open, so a card that asks for 2 cubes takes 2 taps.
  - **Done** is now in that same row and finishes the strike.
  - **Back** switches between Fatigue and Spend.
  - **Any** still lets the app pick a cube, one per tap.
  - **Fewer pop-ups:** a message only appears when the app picked the cube. A cube you tapped already shows on the mat.

  - As an installed app, with gesture and button navigation.
  - In a Chrome tab.
  - In landscape.
- **iPhone 15 and iPhone SE.**
- **3:4 tablet** (768×1024), both ways.
- **16:9 screen** (1280×720), both ways.

- **Light and dark mode:** the strike buttons.
- **Scripted runs:**
  - Fatiguing and spending several cubes in one strike, with Any, Back and Done.
